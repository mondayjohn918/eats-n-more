A day at Eats N More

Doors open at eight, soup by noon, the grill at dusk, a cocktail at nine, a room for the night.

Restaurant, lounge, outdoor bar and guesthouse,
all under one roof on Edo Street since January 2024.

From the kitchen

Five plates people come back for. The full menu has rice, grills, quick bites and the bar.

  1. Melon-seed soup, thick with leaves and assorted meat, served with soft pounded yam.

  2. Green leaves cooked down in palm oil and pepper, with fish and meat, and the swallow of your choice.

  3. Hot, peppery broth with tender meat. It comes properly spicy; ask for it milder if you like.

  4. Meat and fish off the grill in the evening, peppered and smoky, for the table or the bar.

  5. Mixed drinks from the bar, mocktails, and ice cream for the sweet end of the night.
