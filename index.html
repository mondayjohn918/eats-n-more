The kitchen

Native soups and swallow, rice, grills and quick bites, cooked fresh every day. Eat in, or take it home.

  1. Melon-seed soup, thick with leaves and assorted meat, served with soft pounded yam.

  2. Green leaves cooked down in palm oil and pepper, with fish and meat, and the swallow of your choice.

  3. Hot, peppery broth with tender meat. It comes properly spicy; ask for it milder if you like.

  4. Long-grain rice fried with carrots, green beans and liver, served with your choice of protein.

  5. Boiled yam with a rich tomato and egg sauce. The breakfast plate that carries you to lunch.

  6. Meat and fish off the grill in the evening, peppered and smoky, for the table or the bar.

  7. Quick bites for when you want something in the hand: stacked burgers, pizza and shawarma.

  8. Mixed drinks from the bar, mocktails, and ice cream for the sweet end of the night.

Prices move with the market. Ask for today's menu on WhatsApp.
