The menu

West African plates cooked fresh every day, grills in the evening, and a bar that stays open late. Eat in, or take it home.

Soups and swallow Rice and yam Grills Quick bites Bar and sweets

Soups and swallow

The heart of the kitchen. Every soup comes with the swallow of your choice.

  1. Melon-seed soup, thick with leaves and assorted meat.

  2. Green leaves cooked down in palm oil and pepper, with fish and meat.

  3. Hot, peppery broth with tender meat. Properly spicy; ask for it milder.

  4. Pounded yam, eba or semo. Ask what is ready today.

Rice and yam

Plates for lunch and dinner, served with chicken, beef or fish.

  1. Long-grain rice fried with carrots, green beans and liver.

  2. Rice cooked down in a smoky pepper and tomato base, with fried plantain.

  3. Boiled yam with a rich tomato and egg sauce. The breakfast plate that carries you to lunch.

Grills

The grill is lit in the evening. Ask what is on the fire tonight.

  1. Chicken and beef, peppered and smoky, straight off the coals.

  2. Whole fish, scored and spiced, with pepper sauce and onions.

  3. A small plate for the bar: meat tossed in hot pepper sauce.

Quick bites

For when you want something in the hand, or something to share.

  1. A stacked beef burger with melted cheese in a soft brioche bun.

  2. Hot from the oven, to share at the table or take away.

  3. Spiced meat, cabbage and cream, rolled tight and toasted.

  4. Small chops and pastries from the counter. Ask what is fresh.

Bar and sweets

From the lounge bar and the outdoor bar, until ten at night.

  1. Mixed drinks from the lounge bar. Ask for tonight's list.

  2. Fruit and ice, no alcohol, for the whole table.

  3. Cold from the outdoor bar.

  4. For the sweet end of the night.
