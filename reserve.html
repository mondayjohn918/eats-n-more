Before you come

Can I eat in, or only take away?

Both. Sit down in the restaurant or the lounge, or order to take home.

Where exactly are you?

On Edo Street in Ekosodin, a little after the town hall, near the University of Benin. Look for the painted mural wall and the green neon sign.

Is it good for children?

Yes. Families eat with us, and there is ice cream for the end of the meal.

Is the pepper soup very spicy?

It is cooked properly hot. Tell us when you order and we will make it milder.

Can I book the lounge for a party?

Yes. Choose "The lounge" above, tell us the date and how many guests, and add a note about the occasion.
